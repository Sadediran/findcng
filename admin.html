Moderator passcode

Enter the moderator passcode to review submissions. It is set in the Vercel project settings (env ADMIN_PASSCODE).

How to verify a centre

  1. Cross-check the centre name against the Pi-CNG accredited list (pci.gov.ng/conversion-centers.html).
  2. Inspect the certificate: the name matches the submission, the issuing body is NADDC/Pi-CNG, dates are valid, no signs of alteration.
  3. Phone confirmation: call the centre on the submitted number and confirm they are who they claim.
  4. Where possible, physical confirmation by a trusted person in that state.

Approve only when you are satisfied. Reject with a clear reason (e.g. “not found on the Pi-CNG accredited list”, “certificate could not be verified”) so the centre can fix it and re-apply. Approved centres are listed with an “Independently verified” badge and today's date.
